The Process

First, we pull and audit all 3 of your credit reports. We identify every negative, inaccurate, or unverifiable item. Then we draft and send custom dispute letters to the credit bureaus and creditors on your behalf. Bureaus have 30–45 days to respond. We track each response, escalate when needed, and repeat the process until items are removed or corrected.

We dispute with all three major credit bureaus Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion as well as directly with creditors and collection agencies when needed.

We dispute and work to remove: collections, late payments, charge-offs, medical debt, repossessions, bankruptcies, hard inquiries, foreclosures, judgments, and identity-related errors. If an item is inaccurate, outdated, or unverifiable we dispute it.

We can only dispute items that are inaccurate, outdated, or in violation of reporting rules. If an item is 100% accurate and verifiable, it cannot be permanently removed. However many items that appear accurate still have reporting errors, wrong dates, or compliance violations that make them disputable. Our audit identifies all of these.

You get access to a personal client dashboard where you can track every dispute in real-time what's been sent, what's been removed, and what's in progress. You also have a dedicated advisor you can contact by phone, email, or chat anytime.
