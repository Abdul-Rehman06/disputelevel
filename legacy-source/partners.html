Partner Program Frequently Asked Questions

Is there a cost to become a partner?

Absolutely not. Joining the Dispute Levels partner program is at no cost. There are no monthly fees, no setup costs, and no minimum referral requirements.

How do I refer a client?

Once you're approved as a partner, you'll receive a unique referral link and access to the partner portal. You can send clients directly to your link or fill out a quick referral form on their behalf.

How will I know when my client is ready to come back to me?

You'll receive automated email and text notifications when your client hits key milestones including when their score reaches the threshold you need to close the deal.

How long does it take for a client to be credit-ready?

Most clients see their first results within 30–60 days. Full credit restoration typically takes 3–6 months depending on what's on their report. We always keep you updated.

Do you serve Spanish-speaking clients?

Yes. We have dedicated bilingual representatives who serve both English and Spanish speaking clients with the same level of care and professionalism.

What types of negative items do you remove?

We dispute and work to remove collections, late payments, charge-offs, medical debt, bankruptcies, repossessions, hard inquiries, and other inaccurate or outdated negative items.
