Meet Our Founder

Kevin Brown

Founder & CEO, Dispute Levels

Dispute Levels was founded with a simple purpose: helping consumers better understand their credit reports and navigate credit-related challenges with confidence.

Over the years, Kevin has worked with individuals from many different backgrounds who were seeking clarity about their credit reports, financial options, and next steps. Through those experiences, he recognized that many consumers simply needed education, support, and a clear plan to move forward.

That realization led to the creation of Dispute Levels.

Today, our team focuses on providing personalized guidance, educational resources, and administrative support designed to help consumers better understand credit reporting and financial responsibility.

We believe transparency, integrity, and communication should be at the center of every client relationship.

While no company can promise specific outcomes, our commitment is to provide professional support and exceptional service throughout every step of the process.

KNOW YOUR RIGHTS

Important Consumer Information

Consumers have important rights under federal and state law. You have the right to:

Obtain copies of your credit reports
Dispute inaccurate information directly with credit bureaus at no cost
Receive investigation results from credit reporting agencies
Request corrections to inaccurate information
Review your consumer rights under applicable laws

Purchasing our services is not required to exercise these rights.
