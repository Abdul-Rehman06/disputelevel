What Happens on the Call?

Here's exactly what your 30-minute consultation looks like start to finish.

1

We Pull Your Reports

Before or during the call, we access all 3 of your credit bureau reports Equifax, Experian, and TransUnion. You don't need to do this yourself.

2

We Walk Through Everything

Your advisor goes through every negative item on your report with you collections, late payments, charge-offs, hard inquiries, everything. You'll finally understand exactly what's on there and why.

3

We Tell You What We Can Remove

We identify every item that is inaccurate, outdated, or unverifiable and tell you exactly what we can dispute. No vague promises just a specific list of what we go after.

4

You Decide, No Pressure

At the end of the call, we give you a clear plan and a recommended program. You decide if you want to move forward. There is zero pressure to sign up on the call.
