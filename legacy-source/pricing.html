Frequently Asked Questions

Which plan should I choose?

The best plan depends on your individual circumstances, goals, and desired level of support. Our team can help you determine which option may be most appropriate during your consultation.

What exactly do your services include?

Our services may include consultations, educational resources, credit report reviews, administrative support, and personalized action planning.

Do you guarantee results?

No. Because every credit profile is different, we cannot guarantee specific credit score increases, account removals, loan approvals, or other outcomes.

How long does the process take?

Timelines vary based on individual circumstances, responses from credit bureaus and furnishers, and the nature of the issues being reviewed.

Can I upgrade my plan later?

Yes. Existing clients may upgrade to another support level if additional services become appropriate.

Do I need your services to dispute information on my credit report?

No. Consumers have the right to dispute inaccurate information directly with credit reporting agencies at no cost.
