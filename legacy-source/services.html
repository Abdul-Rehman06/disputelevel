Frequently Asked Questions

How does the process work?

We begin with a consultation and review of your credit profile. Based on your circumstances, we provide education, guidance, and administrative support.

Is credit repair legal?

Consumers have legal rights under federal law to dispute potentially inaccurate information appearing on their credit reports.

Can accurate information be removed?

Generally, accurate and verifiable information cannot be removed from a credit report through the dispute process.

How long does the process take?

Every situation is different. Timelines depend on many factors, including responses from credit reporting agencies and furnishers.

Do you guarantee results?

No.

We do not guarantee credit score increases, account removals, loan approvals, or any specific outcome.

Do I need your services to dispute information?

No.

Consumers may dispute inaccurate information directly with credit reporting agencies at no cost.
