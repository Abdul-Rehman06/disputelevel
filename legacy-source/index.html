Know Your Rights

Consumers have important rights under federal law.

You may take action directly with credit reporting agencies and creditors. Our services are optional and results vary.

You Have The Right To:

  • Obtain copies of your credit reports
  • Dispute inaccurate information directly with credit bureaus
  • Receive investigation results from credit reporting agencies
  • Cancel certain agreements within applicable cancellation periods
Consumer Notice

You may dispute inaccurate information directly with the credit bureaus at no cost. Purchasing our services is not required to exercise your legal rights.
